This project showcases the design of a Light Following Robot using Arduino NANO, LDR sensors, and an L293D motor driver.
The robot detects and follows a light source (like a torch or lamp) by comparing intensities from 3 LDR modules (left, center & right) and adjusting motor speeds to move toward the brightest direction. 
It demonstrates the integration of sensors and microcontrollers for autonomous navigation.

### Go plug it in and enjoy !!
## NOTE
If the car is moveing the wrong direction just swap the wiring of the motors!
