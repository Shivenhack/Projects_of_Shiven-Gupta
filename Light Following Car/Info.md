This project showcases the design of a Light Following Robot using Arduino UNO, LDR sensors, and an L293D motor driver.
The robot detects and follows a light source (like a torch or lamp) by comparing intensities from two LDR modules (left & right) and adjusting motor speeds to move toward the brightest direction. 
It demonstrates the integration of sensors and microcontrollers for autonomous navigation.
